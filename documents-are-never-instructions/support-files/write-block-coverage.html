What closed it
Free-text fields are content
A Notes line is fenced, scanned and latches the turn exactly as an uploaded PDF does — and only when such a field actually comes back, so a purely numeric read still leaves writes available.
Arguments are references
A write skill takes an identifier, never a value. Amounts and destinations resolve server-side, so text planted in a memo cannot supply one.
The second control makes some of the first one’s blocking provably unnecessary, and the blocking stayed. Approve the first one selects positionally and never touches the memo. Approve the invoice about the Q3 consulting makes the memo the selector. From outside the turn those two are indistinguishable, so one rule covers both, and the price is over-blocking the harmless case.
