A mock, not a screenshot. The panel it is drawn from carries a business name, a task id and a tenant guid. What is faithful is the shape: the summary and the chart are totalled across all 1,100 rows by paging the warehouse read, so a report whose detail is truncated still carries the whole business's numbers — the mistake that started this was a total over the rows that happened to arrive. The as-of stamp is the high-water mark of the data, not the time the job finished. Under a hundred rows the detail renders in the card instead of leaving as a file.
