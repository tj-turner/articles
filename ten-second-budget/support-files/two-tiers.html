Two tiers, by how they fail
The happy paths are nearly the same and are the boring half. What separates the tiers is who asked for the retry.
Chat tier
10s bound the user can feel
A turn that dies is a retry someone asked for
  • Streams tokens over SSE; somebody is watching it happen.
  • Failure is visible immediately, to the one person who cares.
  • The retry is a human decision, at a time of their choosing.
  • Costs a few seconds and some irritation.
Task tier
2 min wall clock, then cancel
A worker that dies is a redelivery nobody asked for
  • Service Bus delivers at least once, on its own schedule.
  • The duplicate looks exactly like a first delivery.
  • Absorbed by Pending → Running, not by the worker.
  • Arrives against a row that may already be half-finished.

Both commit or neither does, so a loser's result row goes back with the rollback.
Every failure path leaves the result table alone. A composer that throws, a wall clock that expires, a transaction that exhausts its retries — none of them leaves a partial row, so Failed and TimedOut tasks are exactly the ones with no result, and "did this work" stays a single query.
